Watch cars teach themselves to drive

Nobody programs the driving. Each car has a small neural network for a brain, and evolution finds the weights. The goal: finish 3 laps without leaving the road.

  1. SenseEach car shoots distance rays at the road edges. Those distances, plus its speed, are the only things it knows.
  2. ThinkThe numbers flow through the neural network and come out as two decisions: steering and gas/brake.
  3. TryA whole population drives at once, each with a slightly different brain. Generation 1 is pure random chaos.
  4. ScoreThe farther a car gets (and the faster it finishes), the higher its fitness score.
  5. EvolveThe best brains survive, get mixed together and randomly mutated to make the next generation. Repeat.

Experiments to try

  • Set Hidden layers to 0. Can a brain with no hidden neurons still drive?
  • Give the cars only 3 sensors, or a narrow field of view.
  • Crank Mutation strength to the max and watch learning fall apart. Then set it near zero.
  • Turn Grip way down (ice) or Top speed way up, and see if they learn to brake.
  • Set New road every to 1 so they can't memorise a track, then hit Drive it yourself and race them.
