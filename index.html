Watch cars teach themselves to drive

Nobody programs the driving. Each sports car has a small neural network for a brain, and evolution finds the weights. The physics are real: tyre grip, weight transfer, engine power, hills and gravity. The goal: finish 3 laps without leaving the road.

  1. SenseEach car shoots distance rays at the road edges. Those distances, plus its speed, slide and spin, are all it knows.
  2. ThinkThe numbers flow through the neural network and come out as two decisions: steering wheel and gas/brake pedal.
  3. TryA whole population drives at once, each with a slightly different brain. Generation 1 is pure random chaos.
  4. ScoreIt is a time attack. Distance earns points, pace multiplies them, and the quickest finisher wins. Dawdle and you are eliminated.
  5. EvolveThe best brains survive, get mixed together and randomly mutated to make the next generation. Repeat.

Experiments to try

  • Set Hidden layers to 0. Can a brain with no hidden neurons still drive?
  • Give the cars only 3 sensors, or a narrow field of view.
  • Crank Mutation strength to the max and watch learning fall apart. Then set it near zero.
  • Turn Tyre grip down to 0.4 (ice), or Engine power up to 1000 hp, and see if they adapt.
  • Set Gravity to the Moon's 1.6 and watch the cars float through corners.
  • Press Run champion for a timed solo run of the best car, then Test on a new road to see if it really learned to drive.
  • Hit Drive it yourself and try to beat its lap time.
